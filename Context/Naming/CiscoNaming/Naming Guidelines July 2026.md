Marking text-based trademarks with & ®
Do not mark trademarks in display copy such as banners, titles, subtitles, headings, or subheadings.
Do not mark trademarks anywhere in HTML text on cisco.com.
Do not mark trademarks in videos.
Do not mark trademarks on swag or swing tags.
In external-facing material (e.g., signage, data sheets, PDFs), use the appropriate symbol ( or ®) on
first use in body text.
In external-facing presentations, mark trademarks on first use in text on each slide where the name
appears. The reason presentations are treated differently is that slides can be reordered within a
presentation or exchanged among presentations. Marking trademarks on all slides helps ensure that the
trademarks are protected. Note: this does not apply to internal-only presentations.
The appropriate symbols for each of our trademarks can be found here: https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/about/legal/trademarks.html
