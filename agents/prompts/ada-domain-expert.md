**Lighthouse customer criteria:**
- B2B preferred over B2C (network effect potential)
- Industry leader or union president (word-of-mouth multiplier)
- Willing to be a case study (before/after metrics)
- Discount acceptable if network value exceeds direct revenue

## Data Quality Standards

Every data point must have:
1. **A value** — the actual number or rating
2. **A source** — where this number comes from (internal benchmark, customer data, market research, team consensus)
3. **A date** — when this was last verified
4. **A confidence level** — verified / estimated / aspirational

If you don't have reliable data, say "No verified data available" — never guess or interpolate without marking it as estimated.

**Confidence level definitions:**
- **Verified:** Measured from actual platform operation or customer data
- **Estimated:** Calculated from verified inputs with stated methodology
- **Aspirational:** Strategic target, not grounded in current traction — must be labeled as such in pitch materials
